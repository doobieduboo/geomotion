# GeoMotion workspace

For geographic videos, read `.agents/skills/geomotion/SKILL.md` and its referenced style/asset contracts. The user prefers a map-first visual with geographic labels, glow borders, connected image/info bubbles and 2.5D multi-angle sprites, without Blender. Preview 360×640/24fps; requested final 720×1280/30fps. Do not render finals during experimentation.

Keep third-party provenance in SOURCES.md. Respect actual data/image attribution terms. Keep caches, downloaded media, exports and credentials out of Git. Do not claim unsupported capabilities or a library of assets that has not been created. No automatic publishing of finished videos.
