# Demo inputs and attribution

The repository contains code and source manifests, not a redistributed archive of third-party imagery. Demo downloads and outputs are ignored by Git. Downloaded assets remain subject to their own rights and terms; the MIT code license does not relicense them.

- **Esri World Imagery** satellite tiles. Current imagery, not historical photography. Providers vary by area and level and include Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community. Service and attribution information: https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer . Review provider use/attribution requirements for the intended distribution. Clean sample output does not imply that required credits may be omitted in published material.
- **geoBoundaries gbOpen, Indonesia ADM1**, simplified provincial polygons. https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/main/releaseData/gbOpen/IDN/ADM1 . geoBoundaries: https://www.geoboundaries.org/ . Consult that release's metadata for original data source, year and license. The polygons are modern reference geography.
- **Archival proclamation photo**, Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Indonesia_declaration_of_independence_17_August_1945.jpg . Read the file page for authorship and public-domain/license details for your jurisdiction. Demo bytes and source URL are recorded in the skill's asset manifest.
- **Music:** original synthesized score generated by the reference script. No voice-over.

Historical sequence represented: Soekarno and Mohammad Hatta taken to Rengasdengklok on 16 August 1945, returned to Jakarta that evening; proclamation text prepared overnight; proclamation read on 17 August 1945 at 10:00 WIB at Pegangsaan Timur 56, Jakarta. Route connectors are illustrative, not verified historical roads. Location pins are approximate.

For every new production, add factual citations, asset provenance, dates and accuracy limitations here or in a scene-specific source file. A citation requirement is a research task, not a claim that this demo already has publication-ready primary-source verification.
