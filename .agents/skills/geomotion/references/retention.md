# Fast-paced Shorts composition

Use the editorial ranges in [defaults.json](../defaults.json) → `pacing` as starting guides, not hard quotas or measured promises of improved audience retention. This is a production preference; actual retention needs audience analytics. Adapt to topic, names, data complexity and the user's approved script.

## Opening and information rhythm

Establish an understandable hook and geographic anchor promptly. Start with a meaningful question, consequence or visual event rather than a long greeting, logo, blank hold or unrelated headline. Keep the promise aligned with what the clip actually explains; do not create a misleading hook or invent facts for excitement.

Deliver information in short connected phrases with a clear progression and payoff. Respect the approved narrative instead of rewriting it automatically. At each natural information beat, reveal or change something useful: focus/outline, route, marker, photo, concise bubble or evidence-linked effect. Avoid several seconds of empty movement where the story needs a visual explanation. A sustained contextual view is appropriate when viewers need time to read or locate a place.

## Fast voice, understandable map

Follow the TTS preset: conversational, articulate and brisk rather than breathless or theatrical. The target speaking-rate range is guidance, not a fixed generation parameter. Use short punctuation pauses; keep meaningful suspense, sentence boundaries and room to understand names/numbers. Trim accidental dead air and unnecessary post-VO tail, not every breath or thoughtful pause. Do not automatically time-stretch/speed up approved audio.

Use the camera arrival, pre-roll and continuity rules in [composition.md](composition.md#camera-and-continuity). A fast voice does not require fast spinning, repetitive zoom punches or arbitrary cuts. If narration is too dense for the visual, reduce complexity or discuss a script adjustment instead of concealing unreadable frames with more effects.

Subtitles remain phrase-synced with correct punctuation/capitalization, the preset line limit and centered placement. Do not add distracting word-by-word bouncing or flash unreadable text to satisfy a beat timer. Images, cards and labels must have enough screen time for phone viewing. Maintain continuity through working-part boundaries rather than resetting the hook at every part.

## Ending and verification

Deliver the narrative payoff; use a brief relevant question/CTA only when it fits. Avoid unrelated slogans and long silent outros. The camera may widen to the relevant geographic context while the final phrase and fade complete naturally.

At preview stage, inspect opening clarity, reading time, cadence, information progression, camera-to-phrase sync and the last spoken word. Use [quality.md](quality.md) for output checks and [composition.md](composition.md) for geographic continuity. Keep revisions at the preview profile. No extra TTS generations or high-resolution renders merely to test a pacing hypothesis; first reuse approved audio and inspect only affected scene sections.
